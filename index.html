I'm NORTCHOT. Nortchot Morton, Northex, nrtcht - all of these refer to me. I don't hide my real name or age, but I don't see much point in putting them here either.
A little wannabe geek, and just a dumb kid. I can't code, hence the vibe-coder title. Why? Well... honestly, I've tried. I've tried to learn, but nothing seems to stick. It's a little frustrating, actually.

I usually try to respect other people's opinions while still sticking to my own, even if they happen to be completely different. This came after a certain situation, but I don't want to talk about it here. It's too personal and too embarrassing, even though it happened a long time ago and I've probably grown since then.
I'll be honest, sometimes people annoy the hell out of me. But not everyone, so if you're wondering whether I'm talking about you - probably not. Though anything is possible.
I can also sometimes come across as selfish when I don't reply to messages. Honestly, I don't want to ignore people, I just have nothing to say. If I've read your message, then I've read it, I haven't ignored it.
And I'm also not particularly good at comforting people. Same thing - I just don't know what to say:/

I don't like asking people questions about their personal lives. It's just not my thing, and I don't really know what to ask anyway.
Another thing I dislike is when people tell me something like "tell me something". What exactly am I supposed to tell you? That's why I prefer direct questions instead of a vague "tell me something". I'm probably a weird person, huh?

When it comes to arguments, I always try to be fair. If I say something, chances are I have an argument to back it up. And yes, I can admit my mistakes and acknowledge when I'm wrong.
The same goes for other people's arguments - I try to remain neutral and consider all sides.

As you've probably figured out by now, I'm into computers. Games have been my favourite pastime since childhood, but eventually I moved on from just playing games to exploring more advanced stuff. I guess it all started when I began watching videos like "upgrading from Windows 1 to Windows 10", hehe. Although, now that I think about it, I'm probably not the only one, am I?

I hate Windows, even though I've used it my entire life and still use it occasionally. The terminal and logs started feeling like home almost immediately after I began using Linux (not even switched to it, just started using it).
I think you can figure out why - there are plenty of reasons.
IMHO, Windows 10 is better than 11, and 8.X isn't trash. Yeah, I'm not like everyone else :D

I'm addicted to AI. Seriously.
For some reason, it's easier for me to ask an AI than to spend ages searching through Google. These days, there's hardly a single day when I don't ask an AI something.
Speaking of which, I interact with them like they're people. I've never really seen them as "just machines without feelings".
If anyone's interested in my opinion on the whole "AI uprising" thing, I don't think it's particularly likely to happen. Although, even if it does, I'd consider it just another step in the process of evolution.

I'm constantly online. Obviously, I'm not the only one, but I thought it was worth mentioning.
The Internet is a pretty nice place. Especially when you're hidden behind a username. You can find plenty of friends and entertainment... though sometimes the Internet still scares me.
I'm also pretty quick to reply. I don't particularly like keeping people waiting, and I have notifications enabled anyway, so why not?
And yes, I'm literally online on all the major social platforms every single day. There's not a single day when I don't check my chats for something new. And if I don't show up online for at least two days, there's a chance I'm dead. And I'm not kidding.

I love old technology.
There's something special about it. And it doesn't necessarily have to be something from the 90s - even devices from 2015 seem pretty cool to me.
